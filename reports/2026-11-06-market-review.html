Card-by-card findings

1. Umbreon VMAX — Evolving Skies 215/203

LastSold reports a $1,794 English Near Mint reference based on 185 completed sales, with most sales between $1,510 and $2,217 as of 2 November. Its PSA 10 reference is approximately $3,452. CollectSherpa separately records a PSA 10 sale at $3,720 on 26 October.

Interpretation: the best-supported card in this small sample. The raw estimates vary materially between providers, so RareSignal should retain the source range rather than imply a single precise fair value.

Reference sources: LastSold — Umbreon VMAX · CollectSherpa — Umbreon PSA 10

2. Giratina V — Lost Origin 186/196

LastSold reports a $776.39 English Near Mint reference based on 285 completed sales as of 3 November, and a PSA 10 reference around $3,021. ShouldISlabIt reports a $3,050 PSA 10 sold-price median from 27 sales dated 15 October–2 November.

Interpretation: strong raw-sale coverage and a useful independent cross-check for PSA 10. It deserves continued monitoring, but the raw-to-graded multiple is not a grading-profit calculation.

Reference sources: LastSold — Giratina V · ShouldISlabIt — Giratina grading comparison

3. Charizard — Base Set 4/102 Unlimited Holo

Vintage Chase Tracker records a PSA 10 sale at $40,401 on 11 October 2026 and a PSA 10 population of 490. The source explicitly reports too few dated sales to calculate a reliable monthly or 12-month comparison.

Interpretation: do not treat $40,401 as a current median. Raw Near Mint value remains unestablished in this review. Keep Unlimited separate from Shadowless and 1st Edition; they are different printings.

Reference source: Vintage Chase Tracker — Base Set Charizard Unlimited · TCGdex card database

RareSignal assessment

  • Continue with Umbreon VMAX: strongest cross-source evidence in this initial group, but investigate the gap between raw price trackers.
  • Prioritise Giratina V: healthy raw sale coverage and a cross-checkable PSA 10 reference.
  • Defer Charizard valuation: confirm exact printing and gather more condition-matched sales before estimating raw NM or a PSA 10 trend.
