03 / OUR STANDARD

Trust is built
into the method.

We don't treat every listing as a sale, every card with the same name as the same printing, or every price estimate as equally reliable.

  • Completed transactions are distinguished from active listings.
  • Raw condition and graded condition are analysed separately.
  • Market, currency and time period remain visible.
  • Insufficient evidence means no confident investment signal.
