03 / METHODOLOGY

Evidence before conviction.

RareSignal is designed around exact English printings, global market values, completed sales from independent sources and separate raw-card / PSA 10 analysis.

Catalogue metadata identifies a card. It does not establish current market value, authenticity, physical condition or investment merit.
