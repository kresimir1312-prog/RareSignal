03 / METHODOLOGY

Evidence before conviction.

RareSignal is designed around exact English printings, European delivered costs, completed sales from independent sources and separate raw-card / PSA 10 analysis.

Catalogue metadata helps identify a card. It does not establish current market value, authenticity, condition or investment merit.
